# Relata

Relata aims to keep an encounter open to further understanding. Something said in a room should be recoverable later, without anyone having arranged their life around recording it, and remain available for questions its first interpreter could not ask. Its first application is spatial audio: ordinary phones and laptops working as an ad hoc microphone array. Processing and capture components exist; the seamless array and application are still being built.

Each recording is a partial witness. Timing, geometry, speaker identity and meaning remain revisable claims grounded in those witnesses. The spatial engine keeps measurement independent of later textual or psychological interpretation. Correcting a name or rerunning an analysis should add understanding while preserving the observations and previous readings that made it possible. Gaps and competing interpretations stay explicit, and custody—including retention and forgetting—stays with the user.

The larger aim extends this discipline across arbitrary signals into one integrated faculty of situated understanding, whose eventual name remains open. A common, revisable account of a situation would support hypotheses about persons and models of possible continuations. Geodesica's prediction laboratory distinguishes what is unexpected in a situation, what is characteristic of a particular person, and what calls the model of that person into question. It also preserves competing tendencies before they cancel, keeping conflict distinguishable from indifference. A reference model already runs with manually chosen settings. That demonstrates a mechanism; conceptual coherence and explanatory validity against real human behavior require their own evidence. The intended whole joins an instrument of discernment with a cumulative science of how people inhabit and shape their worlds.
